## How Mortgages Work in Denmark

Denmark's mortgage system is unique in Europe — rates are set through the covered bond market (realkreditobligationer), not directly by bank margins. Mortgage banks issue bonds on the capital market, and the bond yield plus a small administration fee becomes the borrower's rate. This creates exceptional transparency and some of the lowest mortgage rates in Europe

Use our [mortgage calculator](/calculator/?country=dk) to see how different rates and terms affect your monthly repayments.

## Types of Mortgages Available

Danish mortgages are primarily offered through the covered bond system. The main types are fast rente (fixed rate) for 10, 15, 20, or 30 years, variabel rente (variable rate) typically linked to short-term bond yields and adjusting every 1-3 years, and capped variable rates (rentetilpasningslån with a cap). Fixed-rate mortgages can be prepaid by buying back the underlying bonds at market price — a unique advantage when rates rise, as bond prices fall and you can effectively repay your mortgage at a discount

## How Much Can You Borrow?

Danish lenders assess affordability through detailed household budget analysis, stress-testing your ability to handle a 5% interest rate increase. Your total housing costs (mortgage, property tax, heating, maintenance) should typically not exceed 35-40% of gross income. Both partners' incomes are considered for joint applications

The maximum loan-to-value in Denmark is Maximum 80% through covered bond lenders (realkredit). A bank supplementary loan (banklån) can cover an additional 15%, bringing total financing to 95%. The remaining 5% minimum must be cash deposit. The bank loan carries a higher interest rate than the covered bond portion. Loan terms extend up to Up to 30 years for covered bond mortgages, with 30-year terms being standard. The first 10 years must include amortisation for LTV above 60%. Bank top-up loans (bridging the gap between the 80% covered bond limit and the purchase price) typically have shorter terms of 10-15 years.

## The Application Process

Contact a realkredit institute directly or through your bank. You need proof of income (lønsedler for the past 3 months), tax returns (årsopgørelse), budget information, details of existing debts, and the purchase agreement. The property is valued by the mortgage bank. The two-layer system means you may deal with both a mortgage bank (for the covered bond loan) and a commercial bank (for the top-up loan)

## Costs and Fees

Administration fees (bidrag) of 0.5-1.5% per year are charged on the outstanding balance — this is the mortgage bank's margin on top of the bond rate. Loan establishment fees (kursskæring/kurtage) apply at drawdown. There are no early repayment penalties for fixed-rate loans, but you repay by buying back bonds at market price, which may be above or below par value

## What Makes Denmark's Mortgage Market Unique

Denmark's covered bond mortgage system is considered one of the most efficient in the world. Because borrowers and investors are directly matched through bonds, there is no bank balance sheet risk, and rates are exceptionally transparent. The ability to repay a fixed-rate mortgage by buying back bonds at market price is unique — if rates have risen significantly since you took the loan, bond prices fall and you can effectively retire your mortgage at a discount, a windfall that exists nowhere else

## Refinancing Your Mortgage

Refinancing in Denmark is done by redeeming your existing bonds and issuing new ones. When interest rates drop, you can refinance to a lower rate (though the old bonds are redeemed at par, which may mean a cost). When rates rise, you can potentially profit by buying back your bonds at below par value. The key consideration is the bidrag (administration fee) and any kurtage (brokerage fee) on the new loan

## Government Schemes and Support

Denmark offers limited direct mortgage subsidies. Mortgage interest is tax-deductible at approximately 33% for amounts up to kr 50,000 per person (lower rate above that threshold). Some municipalities offer discounted building plots to attract new residents. Boligstøtte (housing support) is available for low-income renters but does not apply to owner-occupiers

Check our [government grants guide](/denmark/blog/government-grants-schemes/) for the latest programmes and eligibility details.

## Tips for Getting the Best Deal

Take advantage of the bond buyback mechanism — if rates rise significantly, check whether you can profit by retiring your mortgage early at a discount. Always compare the total annual cost (ÅOP — Årlige Omkostninger i Procent) rather than just the interest rate, as bidrag and fees vary between mortgage banks. Consider mixing fixed and variable elements to balance cost and security
