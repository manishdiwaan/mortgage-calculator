## How Mortgages Work in South Korea

The Bank of Korea's base rate (기준금리) directly influences mortgage rates. Korean mortgage rates are split between bank loans (linked to COFIX — Cost of Funds Index — or the bank's own base rate) and government-backed loans through the Korea Housing Finance Corporation (HF). The government actively uses LTV and DTI regulations to manage housing market demand, adjusting limits by region and property type

Use our [mortgage calculator](/calculator/?country=kr) to see how different rates and terms affect your monthly repayments.

## Types of Mortgages Available

The main types are byeondonggeum-ri (변동금리, variable rate linked to COFIX), gojeong-geum-ri (고정금리, fixed rate for the full term or a partial period), and honhapgeum-ri (혼합금리, mixed — fixed for an initial period then variable). The Korea Housing Finance Corporation offers Bogeumjari Loan (보금자리론), a long-term fixed-rate product with favourable terms for owner-occupiers. Variable rates are common but fixed-rate products have grown in popularity following government encouragement and rate volatility concerns

Read our [mortgage types comparison](/south-korea/blog/mortgage-types-compared/) for a detailed side-by-side analysis of each product.

## How Much Can You Borrow?

The DTI (Debt-to-Income) ratio is strictly regulated — in Seoul, total debt repayments typically cannot exceed 40% of annual income. The DSR (Debt Service Ratio) applies to all borrowers, requiring that annual repayments on all debts (not just housing) do not exceed 40-60% of income depending on the loan amount. These rules significantly impact borrowing capacity, especially in high-priced areas like Seoul

The maximum loan-to-value in South Korea is Highly regulated and varies by region. In speculative overheating zones (투기과열지구) like Gangnam, LTV is capped at 20-40%. In regulated zones (조정대상지역), caps are 50-60%. In non-regulated areas, LTV can reach 60-70%. First-time buyers may access slightly higher LTV in some cases. These regulations are adjusted frequently by the government. Loan terms extend up to Up to 40 years for government-backed loans, 30-35 years for bank loans. Most borrowers choose 20-30 year terms. Longer terms have become more common as property prices have increased relative to incomes.

## The Application Process

Check government-backed loan eligibility through the HF website first. For bank loans, provide 주민등록등본 (resident registration), 소득금액증명원 (income proof — from National Tax Service), 재직증명서 (employment certificate), bank statements, and property documents. The bank conducts a property appraisal. Pre-approval takes 2-5 days. A 법무사 (judicial scrivener) handles the mortgage registration at the district court registry. For government-backed Bogeumjari loans, additional eligibility verification through the HF portal is required

## Costs and Fees

Mortgage registration tax (설정등기 비용) of approximately 0.2% of the loan amount plus judicial scrivener fees. National Housing Fund contribution may apply for certain loan types. Life insurance (사망보험) and fire insurance are typically required. Early repayment penalties of 1-1.5% of the amount repaid apply within the first 3 years for most bank loans, reducing to zero after 3 years. Government-backed loans may have different penalty structures

## What Makes South Korea's Mortgage Market Unique

South Korea's mortgage market is uniquely shaped by government intervention — LTV, DTI, and DSR limits vary by region, property type, and borrower status, and are adjusted frequently as a housing market management tool. The jeonse (전세) rental system also interacts with mortgage demand, as many potential buyers first accumulate wealth through jeonse deposits before transitioning to ownership. The Korea Housing Finance Corporation's Bogeumjari Loan provides attractive long-term fixed rates specifically designed to promote stable home ownership

## Refinancing Your Mortgage

대환대출 (loan conversion/refinancing) has become easier with government initiatives to promote mortgage portability. The early repayment penalty (typically 1-1.5% within 3 years) decreases over time and disappears after 3 years. Online comparison platforms like Banksalad (뱅크샐러드) and Finda (핀다) make it easy to compare rates across lenders. Government-backed loans may offer special refinancing programmes during periods of rate change. Always compare the total cost including fees before switching

## Government Schemes and Support

The Bogeumjari Loan (보금자리론) through HF provides long-term fixed rates for owner-occupiers meeting income and property value caps. The Didimdol Loan (디딤돌대출) offers subsidised rates for first-time buyers and newlyweds. Youth-specific programmes provide reduced rates for borrowers under 34-39. The government periodically adjusts LTV/DTI limits as a housing market tool — relaxations benefit buyers while tightening restricts them. Special programmes for newlywed couples (신혼부부 특례) provide enhanced borrowing limits

Check our [government grants guide](/south-korea/blog/government-grants-schemes/) for the latest programmes and eligibility details.

## Tips for Getting the Best Deal

Check government-backed loan eligibility first — Bogeumjari and Didimdol loans offer significantly better terms than commercial products. LTV and DTI limits vary by region — buying in a non-speculative zone dramatically increases your borrowing capacity. Use Banksalad or Finda to compare rates across all lenders before applying. Keep your DSR low by minimising other debts before applying. After 3 years, early repayment penalties disappear — plan any refinancing around this timeline
