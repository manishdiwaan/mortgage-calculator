## Choosing the Right Mortgage in Denmark

Selecting the right mortgage type is one of the most important financial decisions in the buying process. The wrong choice can cost you tens of thousands over the life of the loan. This guide explains every mortgage type available in Denmark and helps you choose.

## Types of Mortgages Available

Denmark's unique covered bond system creates distinctive mortgage types. **Fast rente (fixed rate)** locks your rate for 10, 15, 20, or 30 years through covered bonds — you can prepay by buying back your bonds at market price, which is a unique feature. **Rentetilpasningslån (adjustable rate)** resets every 1, 3, or 5 years based on new bond auctions — lower initial rate but renewal risk. **Capped variable** adds a maximum rate ceiling to the adjustable product, providing a safety net. The covered bond system means rates are set by the capital market, not bank margins, creating exceptional transparency

## Head-to-Head Comparison

Fixed rate gives 30 years of certainty at very competitive rates by European standards. Adjustable rates start 0.5-1.5% lower but carry renewal risk. The unique bond buyback feature means fixed-rate borrowers can profit if rates rise (bonds fall below par, letting you retire your mortgage at a discount). Capped variable offers a compromise — lower cost with a ceiling on risk

## Which Type Is Right for You?

Your ideal mortgage type depends on your risk tolerance, financial stability, and market outlook.

Choose **fixed rate** if you value payment certainty, are on a tight budget with little room for payment increases, or believe rates will rise. Choose **variable rate** if you can absorb payment increases, want the lowest possible initial cost, or believe rates will stay stable or fall.

Consider your loan term carefully. Shorter terms mean higher monthly payments but dramatically lower total interest. Our [mortgage guide](/denmark/mortgage-guide/) provides a detailed analysis of how term length affects total cost.

## Expert Tips for Denmark

Take advantage of the bond buyback mechanism — if rates rise significantly, you can retire your fixed-rate mortgage at a discount by buying back the underlying bonds at below par value. Always compare the total annual cost (ÅOP) rather than just the interest rate, as bidrag and fees vary between mortgage banks
