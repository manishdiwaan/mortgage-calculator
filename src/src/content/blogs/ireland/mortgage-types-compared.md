## Choosing the Right Mortgage in Ireland

Selecting the right mortgage type is one of the most important financial decisions in the buying process. The wrong choice can cost you tens of thousands over the life of the loan. This guide explains every mortgage type available in Ireland and helps you choose.

## Types of Mortgages Available

The Irish market offers three main types. **Fixed rate** locks your rate for 1-10 years (some lenders now offer up to 30-year fixed) — increasingly popular and the majority choice for new borrowers. **Standard Variable Rate (SVR)** is set by the lender and can change at any time — historically the most expensive option. **Tracker rate** was linked directly to the ECB rate plus a margin — no longer available for new borrowers but existing trackers are extremely valuable (rates as low as ECB+0.5%). **Green mortgages** offer discounted rates for energy-efficient homes (BER A or B rated)

## Head-to-Head Comparison

Fixed rates (3-5 year) are the most popular choice for new borrowers — they provide certainty in a market with limited competition. SVR is almost always more expensive and should be avoided if possible. If you have a legacy tracker mortgage, never give it up — it cannot be replaced. Green mortgages reward energy-efficient properties with 0.1-0.2% rate discounts

## Which Type Is Right for You?

Your ideal mortgage type depends on your risk tolerance, financial stability, and market outlook.

Choose **fixed rate** if you value payment certainty, are on a tight budget with little room for payment increases, or believe rates will rise. Choose **variable rate** if you can absorb payment increases, want the lowest possible initial cost, or believe rates will stay stable or fall.

Consider your loan term carefully. Shorter terms mean higher monthly payments but dramatically lower total interest. Our [mortgage guide](/ireland/mortgage-guide/) provides a detailed analysis of how term length affects total cost.

## Expert Tips for Ireland

The LTI cap (4x gross income for FTBs) is usually the binding constraint — calculate this before starting your search. Always check Avant Money and Finance Ireland alongside the main banks for fixed rates. If buying a new build, apply for Help to Buy before signing the contract. Mortgage protection insurance is mandatory but can be sourced independently for cheaper rates
