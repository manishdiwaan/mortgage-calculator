## Choosing the Right Mortgage in Saudi Arabia

Selecting the right mortgage type is one of the most important financial decisions in the buying process. The wrong choice can cost you tens of thousands over the life of the loan. This guide explains every mortgage type available in Saudi Arabia and helps you choose.

## Types of Mortgages Available

Saudi Arabia offers both conventional and Islamic financing. **Murabaha** is the most common — the bank buys the property and sells it to you at a marked-up price, with payments spread over the term. **Ijara** is a lease-to-own structure — the bank buys the property and leases it to you, with ownership transferring at the end. **Musharaka Mutanaqisa** is a declining partnership — you and the bank co-own the property, with your share increasing as you make payments. **REDF profit-free financing** provides up to SAR 500,000 at zero profit (interest) for eligible Saudi nationals. Fixed and variable profit rate options are available for Islamic products

## Head-to-Head Comparison

REDF profit-free financing is always the best option if eligible — zero profit on up to SAR 500,000. For the commercial portion, Murabaha is the simplest structure with clear fixed payments. Ijara offers more flexibility. Fixed-rate products provide certainty but may cost slightly more. The key decision is between REDF-only (for eligible properties within the limit) or REDF + commercial bank (for higher-value properties)

## Which Type Is Right for You?

Your ideal mortgage type depends on your risk tolerance, financial stability, and market outlook.

Choose **fixed rate** if you value payment certainty, are on a tight budget with little room for payment increases, or believe rates will rise. Choose **variable rate** if you can absorb payment increases, want the lowest possible initial cost, or believe rates will stay stable or fall.

Consider your loan term carefully. Shorter terms mean higher monthly payments but dramatically lower total interest. Our [mortgage guide](/saudi-arabia/mortgage-guide/) provides a detailed analysis of how term length affects total cost.

## Expert Tips for Saudi Arabia

Check Sakani first — if eligible, REDF profit-free financing saves hundreds of thousands of riyals over the life of the loan. Understand the salary assignment requirement before committing. Compare offers from at least 3 banks for the commercial portion. Ensure you understand the total cost including takaful insurance
