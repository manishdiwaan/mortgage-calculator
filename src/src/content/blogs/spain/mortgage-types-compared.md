## Choosing the Right Mortgage in Spain

Selecting the right mortgage type is one of the most important financial decisions in the buying process. The wrong choice can cost you tens of thousands over the life of the loan. This guide explains every mortgage type available in Spain and helps you choose.

## Types of Mortgages Available

Spain offers three main types, shaped by the strong consumer protections of the 2019 Mortgage Law. **Hipoteca a tipo variable** links to Euribor plus a spread, reviewing annually or semi-annually — was dominant until the 2022-2023 Euribor spike. **Hipoteca a tipo fijo** locks the rate for the full term — now the most popular product. **Hipoteca a tipo mixto** is fixed initially (3-10 years) then converts to variable. The 2019 law shifted most costs to the bank, capped early repayment charges, and requires a mandatory 10-day reflection period with notary verification

## Head-to-Head Comparison

Fixed rate has become the majority choice following the Euribor spike — providing 20-30 year certainty. Variable rate is cheaper when Euribor is low but the 2022-2023 surge demonstrated the risk. Mixed (3-5 year fixed then variable) offers a compromise. The 2019 law's consumer protections apply to all types. Early repayment penalties are capped at 0.25% for variable and 2% for fixed

## Which Type Is Right for You?

Your ideal mortgage type depends on your risk tolerance, financial stability, and market outlook.

Choose **fixed rate** if you value payment certainty, are on a tight budget with little room for payment increases, or believe rates will rise. Choose **variable rate** if you can absorb payment increases, want the lowest possible initial cost, or believe rates will stay stable or fall.

Consider your loan term carefully. Shorter terms mean higher monthly payments but dramatically lower total interest. Our [mortgage guide](/spain/mortgage-guide/) provides a detailed analysis of how term length affects total cost.

## Expert Tips for Spain

Use iAhorro or HelpMyCash to compare — Spanish rates vary significantly between lenders. Negotiate vinculaciones carefully — they reduce your rate but commit you to bank products that may be overpriced. The 10-day mandatory reflection period is your right — use the FEIN to compare offers from multiple banks before signing. If on variable rate post-Euribor spike, explore novación (renegotiation) to fixed with your existing bank
