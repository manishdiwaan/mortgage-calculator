## Government Grants and Schemes for Property Buyers in Japan

Buying property is one of the largest financial commitments you will make. Government programmes in Japan can significantly reduce costs through grants, tax exemptions, subsidised financing, and other support mechanisms. This guide covers every major programme available in 2026.

## Current Programmes and Eligibility

Housing loan tax deduction (住宅ローン控除) of 0.7% of remaining balance annually for 13 years (new homes) or 10 years (existing). Flat 35S provides 0.25% rate discount for first 5-10 years on quality-certified homes. ZEH subsidies for net-zero energy houses. Sumai-Kyufu-Kin grants based on income level. Local municipality subsidies vary — some offer cash grants for relocating to rural areas. Renovation grants for earthquake resistance and energy efficiency upgrades

## How to Apply

Eligibility criteria, application windows, and benefit amounts change periodically. Always verify current terms directly with the administering body before making purchase decisions based on expected support. Apply for relevant programmes early in your buying process — some have limited funding or processing times that require advance planning.

## Combining Multiple Benefits

In Japan, it is often possible to combine multiple government programmes. For example, first-time buyer tax relief can be combined with subsidised financing or grants. Calculate the total benefit package before finalising your budget — the combined value can be substantial.

## Tax Benefits of Property Ownership

Beyond direct grants and subsidies, property ownership in Japan often comes with ongoing tax advantages. These may include deductions on mortgage interest, reduced rates on primary residences, or exemptions on capital gains.
