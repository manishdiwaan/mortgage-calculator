## Choosing the Right Mortgage in Belgium

Selecting the right mortgage type is one of the most important financial decisions in the buying process. The wrong choice can cost you tens of thousands over the life of the loan. This guide explains every mortgage type available in Belgium and helps you choose.

## Types of Mortgages Available

Belgium offers three main mortgage types. **Vaste rentevoet (fixed rate)** locks your rate for the full term — this is the most popular choice, providing complete payment certainty. Monthly payments stay identical from first to last. **Variabele rentevoet (variable rate)** adjusts periodically (annually, every 3 years, or every 5 years) based on market conditions. Belgian law provides important protection: your variable rate can never more than double from its initial level, creating a legal safety net. **Semi-vaste rentevoet (semi-fixed)** offers a fixed rate for an initial period (typically 5-10 years) before converting to variable. This gives initial certainty with the possibility of benefiting from rate decreases later

## Head-to-Head Comparison

Fixed rate offers maximum certainty — your payment never changes. Variable rate starts lower but carries risk of increases (capped by law at double the initial rate). Semi-fixed gives a middle ground. In low-rate environments, locking in a fixed rate is generally recommended. In high-rate environments, variable may save money if rates are expected to decline

## Which Type Is Right for You?

Your ideal mortgage type depends on your risk tolerance, financial stability, and market outlook.

Choose **fixed rate** if you value payment certainty, are on a tight budget with little room for payment increases, or believe rates will rise. Choose **variable rate** if you can absorb payment increases, want the lowest possible initial cost, or believe rates will stay stable or fall.

Consider your loan term carefully. Shorter terms mean higher monthly payments but dramatically lower total interest. Our [mortgage guide](/belgium/mortgage-guide/) provides a detailed analysis of how term length affects total cost.

## Expert Tips for Belgium

Belgian variable rate mortgages have a unique legal protection — the rate can never more than double from the initial level. This cap makes variable rates less risky in Belgium than in most countries. Always compare the AEIR (Annual Effective Interest Rate) which includes all costs, not just the headline rate
