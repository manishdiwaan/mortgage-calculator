## Choosing the Right Mortgage in Italy

Selecting the right mortgage type is one of the most important financial decisions in the buying process. The wrong choice can cost you tens of thousands over the life of the loan. This guide explains every mortgage type available in Italy and helps you choose.

## Types of Mortgages Available

Italy offers several mortgage types. **Tasso fisso (fixed rate)** locks your rate for the full term — the most popular product, chosen by over 70% of Italian borrowers. **Tasso variabile (variable rate)** is linked to Euribor and adjusts periodically — lower initial cost but rate risk. **Tasso variabile con cap (capped variable)** has a maximum rate ceiling, providing downside protection. **Tasso misto (mixed)** allows switching between fixed and variable during the loan term. Italy's **surroga** (free mortgage transfer) means you can switch to any lender at zero cost — a uniquely powerful consumer right

## Head-to-Head Comparison

Fixed rate dominates the Italian market for good reason — it provides certainty over 20-30 years. Variable rate saves money when Euribor is low but the 2022-2023 spike showed the risk. Capped variable offers a middle ground but the cap premium reduces the saving versus fixed. The surroga option means choosing variable is less risky than in other countries — if rates spike, you can switch to fixed at another bank for free

## Which Type Is Right for You?

Your ideal mortgage type depends on your risk tolerance, financial stability, and market outlook.

Choose **fixed rate** if you value payment certainty, are on a tight budget with little room for payment increases, or believe rates will rise. Choose **variable rate** if you can absorb payment increases, want the lowest possible initial cost, or believe rates will stay stable or fall.

Consider your loan term carefully. Shorter terms mean higher monthly payments but dramatically lower total interest. Our [mortgage guide](/italy/mortgage-guide/) provides a detailed analysis of how term length affects total cost.
