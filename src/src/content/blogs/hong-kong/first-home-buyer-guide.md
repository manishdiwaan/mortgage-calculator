## Your First Home in Hong Kong — Where to Start

Buying your first home in Hong Kong is one of the biggest financial decisions you will make. The process can feel overwhelming, but breaking it down into clear steps makes it manageable. This guide covers everything from saving your deposit to collecting the keys.

## Saving for Your Deposit

Most lenders in Hong Kong require a deposit of 10-50% of the property price. The more you save, the better your loan terms and the less you pay in additional costs.

Build savings consistently — banks assess your savings pattern over 6+ months. Consider government-subsidised housing schemes if eligible. Maintain low credit card utilisation and clear outstanding debts to improve your TU credit score

## Government Support for First-Time Buyers

The Home Ownership Scheme (HOS) sells flats at 30-40% below market value. The Mortgage Insurance Programme (MIP) enables up to 90% LTV. The White Form Secondary Market Scheme allows buying HOS resale flats. CPF Housing Grants equivalent does not exist, but these schemes represent significant government support

Check our [government grants guide](/hong-kong/blog/government-grants-schemes/) for the full list of current programmes and eligibility requirements.

## Getting Pre-Approved for a Mortgage

Pre-approval tells you exactly how much you can borrow and shows sellers you are a serious buyer. In Hong Kong, the process works as follows.

Apply to 3-4 banks simultaneously — Hong Kong banks actively compete with rate offers and cash rebates. Get pre-approval before property hunting. Consider a mortgage broker like mReferral for access to the best current deals

Use our [mortgage calculator](/calculator/?country=hk) to model different scenarios before applying. Read our [mortgage types guide](/hong-kong/blog/mortgage-types-compared/) to understand which loan product suits your situation.

## Understanding the Full Cost of Buying

The purchase price is just the beginning. Budget for additional costs that can add significantly to your total outlay.

Ad Valorem Stamp Duty at Scale 2 rates for first-time permanent residents: HK$100 to 4. Read our [buying costs guide](/hong-kong/blog/buying-costs-explained/) for a complete breakdown of every expense.

## Common First-Time Buyer Mistakes

Not applying to multiple banks simultaneously (missing better cash rebates), underestimating ABSD stamp duty if you are not a first-time buyer, rushing into off-plan purchases without understanding completion risk, and not checking the 49% foreign quota for buildings if applicable

Avoid these pitfalls by taking your time, doing thorough research, and leaning on qualified professionals throughout the process. Our [property inspection checklist](/hong-kong/blog/property-inspection-checklist/) covers what to check before committing.
