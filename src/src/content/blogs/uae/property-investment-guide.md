## Property Investment Guide — UAE 2026

Property investment can build long-term wealth through rental income and capital appreciation. However, it requires careful analysis of yields, taxes, financing costs, and market dynamics. This guide covers everything you need to know about investing in property in UAE.

## Rental Yields and Market Overview

UAE rental yields average 5-8%, among the highest globally for comparable markets. Zero income tax means gross yield equals net yield (before service charges). DLD registration of 4% is the main transaction cost. Service charges of AED 15-30/sqft/year significantly affect net returns. Capital gains are not taxed. The market is cyclical with strong off-plan activity. Golden Visa at AED 2M+ adds residency value. RERA rental index determines permissible rent increases for existing tenancies

## Financing Investment Property

Investment property financing differs from owner-occupier mortgages. Lenders typically require higher deposits (20-40%), charge higher interest rates (0.5-2% premium), and apply stricter affordability criteria. Rental income may be partially factored into the assessment, but expect conservative treatment. Use our [mortgage calculator](/calculator/?country=ae) to model investment scenarios.

## Tax Implications

Understanding the full tax picture is critical for investment returns. Rental income taxation, capital gains treatment, deductible expenses, and stamp duty or transfer tax surcharges on additional properties all affect your net return. ## Due Diligence for Investment

Investment due diligence goes beyond a standard purchase. Research local rental demand, vacancy rates, tenant quality, maintenance requirements, and comparable rental values.
