## Choosing the Right Mortgage in UAE

Selecting the right mortgage type is one of the most important financial decisions in the buying process. The wrong choice can cost you tens of thousands over the life of the loan. This guide explains every mortgage type available in UAE and helps you choose.

## Types of Mortgages Available

UAE offers conventional and Islamic options. **Conventional fixed rate** locks the rate for 1-5 years then reverts to EIBOR-based variable — the initial fixed period is the headline rate. **Conventional variable** is linked to EIBOR plus a bank margin — fluctuates with market rates. **Ijara (Islamic)** is a lease-to-own structure where the bank buys the property and leases it to you — economically similar to conventional. **Murabaha (Islamic)** is a cost-plus sale structure. **Diminishing Musharaka (Islamic)** is a declining partnership. Both conventional and Islamic products are available from most banks at similar effective rates

## Head-to-Head Comparison

Islamic and conventional products are usually priced identically from the same bank — choose based on personal/religious preference. Fixed-rate products (1-5 year initial) provide budgeting certainty for the initial period but revert to variable. Variable EIBOR-linked products start lower but carry immediate rate risk. The reversion rate after the initial fixed period is the key long-term cost — compare this, not just the promotional rate

## Which Type Is Right for You?

Your ideal mortgage type depends on your risk tolerance, financial stability, and market outlook.

Choose **fixed rate** if you value payment certainty, are on a tight budget with little room for payment increases, or believe rates will rise. Choose **variable rate** if you can absorb payment increases, want the lowest possible initial cost, or believe rates will stay stable or fall.

Consider your loan term carefully. Shorter terms mean higher monthly payments but dramatically lower total interest. Our [mortgage guide](/uae/mortgage-guide/) provides a detailed analysis of how term length affects total cost.

## Expert Tips for UAE

Use a mortgage broker (Holo, Mortgage Finder, Huspy) — they access all banks for free. Negotiate the processing fee aggressively — it is often waived or reduced. Compare the reversion rate, not just the initial fixed rate. If buying off-plan, understand that bank LTV is limited to 50% during construction — ensure you have cash or developer payment plan for the rest. Refinance actively — the 1% early settlement cap means switching costs are manageable
