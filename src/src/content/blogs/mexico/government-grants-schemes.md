## Government Grants and Schemes for Property Buyers in Mexico

Buying property is one of the largest financial commitments you will make. Government programmes in Mexico can significantly reduce costs through grants, tax exemptions, subsidised financing, and other support mechanisms. This guide covers every major programme available in 2026.

## Current Programmes and Eligibility

INFONAVIT credits based on employer contributions (5% of salary) at subsidised rates. FOVISSSTE for government employees. Cofinavit combines INFONAVIT with bank financing for higher-value properties. Federal subsidies for lower-income buyers purchasing affordable housing reduce the purchase price directly. CONAVI programmes target social housing. State-level programmes vary by entity

## How to Apply

Eligibility criteria, application windows, and benefit amounts change periodically. Always verify current terms directly with the administering body before making purchase decisions based on expected support. Apply for relevant programmes early in your buying process — some have limited funding or processing times that require advance planning.

## Combining Multiple Benefits

In Mexico, it is often possible to combine multiple government programmes. For example, first-time buyer tax relief can be combined with subsidised financing or grants. Calculate the total benefit package before finalising your budget — the combined value can be substantial.

## Tax Benefits of Property Ownership

Beyond direct grants and subsidies, property ownership in Mexico often comes with ongoing tax advantages. These may include deductions on mortgage interest, reduced rates on primary residences, or exemptions on capital gains.
