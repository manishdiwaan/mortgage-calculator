## Choosing the Right Mortgage in Portugal

Selecting the right mortgage type is one of the most important financial decisions in the buying process. The wrong choice can cost you tens of thousands over the life of the loan. This guide explains every mortgage type available in Portugal and helps you choose.

## Types of Mortgages Available

Portuguese mortgages offer three main types. **Taxa variável (variable rate)** is linked to Euribor (6 or 12-month) plus a bank spread — historically the most popular product. **Taxa fixa (fixed rate)** locks the rate for the full term or a partial period — gained significant popularity after the 2022-2023 Euribor spike. **Taxa mista (mixed)** provides a fixed rate for an initial period (typically 3-10 years) then converts to variable. Portuguese banks commonly offer **bonificações** (spread reductions) for subscribing to linked products (insurance, payroll domiciliation, credit cards)

## Head-to-Head Comparison

Variable rate was long dominant but the Euribor spike changed preferences dramatically — many borrowers shifted to fixed or mixed rates. Fixed provides certainty but at a premium. Mixed (3-5 year fixed then variable) offers a compromise. The bonificação system means your effective rate depends heavily on how many linked products you subscribe to — always calculate the net benefit

## Which Type Is Right for You?

Your ideal mortgage type depends on your risk tolerance, financial stability, and market outlook.

Choose **fixed rate** if you value payment certainty, are on a tight budget with little room for payment increases, or believe rates will rise. Choose **variable rate** if you can absorb payment increases, want the lowest possible initial cost, or believe rates will stay stable or fall.

Consider your loan term carefully. Shorter terms mean higher monthly payments but dramatically lower total interest. Our [mortgage guide](/portugal/mortgage-guide/) provides a detailed analysis of how term length affects total cost.

## Expert Tips for Portugal

Use ComparaJá.pt or Doutor Finanças to compare all lenders. Negotiate the spread aggressively — Portuguese banks have wide ranges and expect negotiation. Evaluate vinculações carefully — sometimes the bank's insurance is overpriced and the spread reduction does not justify the cost. If on variable rate, monitor ECB decisions closely and consider converting to fixed if you expect further rate increases
