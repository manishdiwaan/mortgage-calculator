## Choosing the Right Mortgage in Canada

Selecting the right mortgage type is one of the most important financial decisions in the buying process. The wrong choice can cost you tens of thousands over the life of the loan. This guide explains every mortgage type available in Canada and helps you choose.

## Types of Mortgages Available

Canadian mortgages are unique because the **term** (when your rate expires) is much shorter than the **amortisation** (when the loan is fully paid). A **fixed-rate mortgage** locks in your rate for the term (typically 5 years) — the most popular choice, providing payment certainty. A **variable-rate mortgage** fluctuates with the prime rate — lower initial cost but payments change when the Bank of Canada moves rates. An **adjustable-rate mortgage (ARM)** adjusts your payment amount with prime, while variable-rate may keep payments steady but adjust the principal/interest split. At term renewal (every 1-5 years), you renegotiate your rate — unlike the US 30-year fixed, Canadian rates change regularly

## Head-to-Head Comparison

The 5-year fixed is Canada's most popular product — reliable payments and no surprises. Variable/adjustable rates have historically been cheaper over time but carry risk when rates rise sharply (as seen in 2022-2023). Shorter terms (1-3 year fixed) offer lower rates but more frequent renewals. The key Canadian insight: your rate changes every 1-5 years regardless, so the long-term difference between fixed and variable is smaller than in countries with 25-30 year fixed rates

## Which Type Is Right for You?

Your ideal mortgage type depends on your risk tolerance, financial stability, and market outlook.

Choose **fixed rate** if you value payment certainty, are on a tight budget with little room for payment increases, or believe rates will rise. Choose **variable rate** if you can absorb payment increases, want the lowest possible initial cost, or believe rates will stay stable or fall.

Consider your loan term carefully. Shorter terms mean higher monthly payments but dramatically lower total interest. Our [mortgage guide](/canada/mortgage-guide/) provides a detailed analysis of how term length affects total cost.

## Expert Tips for Canada

Use a mortgage broker for your first purchase — they compare dozens of lenders for free. At renewal, never just accept your bank's first offer — always get competing quotes. Consider accelerated bi-weekly payments, which make one extra monthly payment per year and can cut 3-4 years off a 25-year amortisation
