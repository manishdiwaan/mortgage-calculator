## Property Investment Guide — India 2026

Property investment can build long-term wealth through rental income and capital appreciation. However, it requires careful analysis of yields, taxes, financing costs, and market dynamics. This guide covers everything you need to know about investing in property in India.

## Rental Yields and Market Overview

Indian rental yields average 2-4% in major cities. Rental income above ₹2.5 lakh (old regime) is taxed at slab rate with 30% standard deduction for repairs. Capital gains: short-term (under 2 years) at slab rate, long-term at 20% with indexation. Section 54 allows exemption by reinvesting in another property. TDS of 1% on property transactions above ₹50 lakh. NRIs face 20.8% TDS on long-term capital gains

## Financing Investment Property

Investment property financing differs from owner-occupier mortgages. Lenders typically require higher deposits (20-40%), charge higher interest rates (0.5-2% premium), and apply stricter affordability criteria. Rental income may be partially factored into the assessment, but expect conservative treatment. Use our [mortgage calculator](/calculator/?country=in) to model investment scenarios.

## Tax Implications

Understanding the full tax picture is critical for investment returns. Rental income taxation, capital gains treatment, deductible expenses, and stamp duty or transfer tax surcharges on additional properties all affect your net return. ## Due Diligence for Investment

Investment due diligence goes beyond a standard purchase. Research local rental demand, vacancy rates, tenant quality, maintenance requirements, and comparable rental values.
