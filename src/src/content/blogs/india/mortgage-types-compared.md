## Choosing the Right Mortgage in India

Selecting the right mortgage type is one of the most important financial decisions in the buying process. The wrong choice can cost you tens of thousands over the life of the loan. This guide explains every mortgage type available in India and helps you choose.

## Types of Mortgages Available

Indian home loans are predominantly variable rate. **Floating rate (repo-linked)** adjusts quarterly with the RBI repo rate — this is the most common product since 2019 when RBI mandated external benchmark linking. Rates move transparently with RBI decisions. **Fixed rate** locks your rate for the full term or a partial period — typically 0.5-1% higher than floating and offered by fewer lenders. **Hybrid/semi-fixed** provides a fixed rate for 2-5 years then converts to floating. The key Indian advantage: **zero prepayment penalty on floating-rate loans** (RBI mandated), giving borrowers exceptional flexibility

## Head-to-Head Comparison

Floating rate is the default choice for Indian borrowers — it is cheaper, and the zero prepayment penalty means you can always pay extra or switch lenders for free. Fixed rate provides certainty but costs more and may carry prepayment penalties of up to 2%. Hybrid offers initial certainty at a lower premium than full fixed. Given India's rate cycle, floating rate with active balance transfers when better rates are available is the most cost-effective strategy

## Which Type Is Right for You?

Your ideal mortgage type depends on your risk tolerance, financial stability, and market outlook.

Choose **fixed rate** if you value payment certainty, are on a tight budget with little room for payment increases, or believe rates will rise. Choose **variable rate** if you can absorb payment increases, want the lowest possible initial cost, or believe rates will stay stable or fall.

Consider your loan term carefully. Shorter terms mean higher monthly payments but dramatically lower total interest. Our [mortgage guide](/india/mortgage-guide/) provides a detailed analysis of how term length affects total cost.

## Expert Tips for India

Maintain a CIBIL score above 750 for the best rates — even a small improvement from 720 to 760 can save 0.25% on your rate. Use balance transfer actively — check competing rates annually and switch if the differential exceeds 0.25% (free for floating rate loans). Make prepayments whenever possible — at Indian rates, even ₹50,000 extra per year can save lakhs
