## Choosing the Right Mortgage in Singapore

Selecting the right mortgage type is one of the most important financial decisions in the buying process. The wrong choice can cost you tens of thousands over the life of the loan. This guide explains every mortgage type available in Singapore and helps you choose.

## Types of Mortgages Available

Singapore has a distinctive dual-market system. **HDB concessionary loan** is fixed at CPF OA rate +0.1% (currently 2.6%) with no lock-in, no early repayment penalty, and full CPF usage — offered directly by HDB for eligible buyers. **Bank SORA-linked loan** adjusts with the Singapore Overnight Rate Average — lower initial rate but variable and typically includes a 2-3 year lock-in. **Bank fixed-rate package** locks the rate for 2-5 years then reverts to SORA-based floating — offers short-term certainty. Most bank products include lock-in periods with early repayment penalties of 1.5%

## Head-to-Head Comparison

HDB loan offers unmatched stability (2.6% fixed, no lock-in, penalty-free prepayment) but at a slightly higher rate than promotional bank packages. Bank loans offer lower initial rates (often 1-2% below HDB) but carry rate risk and lock-in penalties. The key trade-off: HDB loan stability versus bank loan cost savings. Once you switch from HDB to bank, you cannot switch back. For BTO buyers, the HDB loan is often the safer choice given the long holding period

## Which Type Is Right for You?

Your ideal mortgage type depends on your risk tolerance, financial stability, and market outlook.

Choose **fixed rate** if you value payment certainty, are on a tight budget with little room for payment increases, or believe rates will rise. Choose **variable rate** if you can absorb payment increases, want the lowest possible initial cost, or believe rates will stay stable or fall.

Consider your loan term carefully. Shorter terms mean higher monthly payments but dramatically lower total interest. Our [mortgage guide](/singapore/mortgage-guide/) provides a detailed analysis of how term length affects total cost.

## Expert Tips for Singapore

For HDB buyers: carefully compare the HDB loan (2.6% fixed, zero penalties, full CPF usage) versus bank loans (lower rate but lock-in and risk). Remember you cannot switch back from bank to HDB. Use CPF for monthly payments but track your accrued interest exposure. If choosing a bank loan, refinance every 2-3 years when the lock-in expires — this is standard practice in Singapore
