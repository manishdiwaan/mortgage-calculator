## Property Investment Guide — Singapore 2026

Property investment can build long-term wealth through rental income and capital appreciation. However, it requires careful analysis of yields, taxes, financing costs, and market dynamics. This guide covers everything you need to know about investing in property in Singapore.

## Rental Yields and Market Overview

Singapore rental yields average 3-4% for condominiums. ABSD makes investment expensive: 20% for second property (citizens), 30% for third+, 60% for foreigners. Rental income is taxed at personal rates (0-22%). Property tax is progressive: owner-occupier rates of 0-16%, non-owner-occupier rates of 12-36%. Capital gains are not taxed in Singapore (no CGT). SSD (Seller's Stamp Duty) applies if sold within 3 years of purchase

## Financing Investment Property

Investment property financing differs from owner-occupier mortgages. Lenders typically require higher deposits (20-40%), charge higher interest rates (0.5-2% premium), and apply stricter affordability criteria. Rental income may be partially factored into the assessment, but expect conservative treatment. Use our [mortgage calculator](/calculator/?country=sg) to model investment scenarios.

## Tax Implications

Understanding the full tax picture is critical for investment returns. Rental income taxation, capital gains treatment, deductible expenses, and stamp duty or transfer tax surcharges on additional properties all affect your net return. ## Due Diligence for Investment

Investment due diligence goes beyond a standard purchase. Research local rental demand, vacancy rates, tenant quality, maintenance requirements, and comparable rental values.
