## Choosing the Right Mortgage in Norway

Selecting the right mortgage type is one of the most important financial decisions in the buying process. The wrong choice can cost you tens of thousands over the life of the loan. This guide explains every mortgage type available in Norway and helps you choose.

## Types of Mortgages Available

Norwegian mortgages are predominantly variable rate. **Flytende rente (floating rate)** adjusts with Norges Bank's policy rate — chosen by approximately 80-90% of Norwegian borrowers. This is the cheapest option in most market conditions. **Fastrente (fixed rate)** locks the rate for 3, 5, or 10 years — provides certainty but at a premium. **Rammelån (credit line)** allows borrowing against existing equity with flexible drawdown. All Norwegian mortgages are fully amortising under current regulations — interest-only periods are generally not available

## Head-to-Head Comparison

Floating rate dominates the Norwegian market for good reason — it is the cheapest option and Norges Bank's transparent communication gives borrowers visibility into rate direction. Fixed rate provides certainty but has historically cost more over time. The 3-year fixed period is the most common fixed option, balancing cost and security. Given Norway's 5x income debt cap and stress test requirements, the choice between fixed and variable affects how much you can borrow

## Which Type Is Right for You?

Your ideal mortgage type depends on your risk tolerance, financial stability, and market outlook.

Choose **fixed rate** if you value payment certainty, are on a tight budget with little room for payment increases, or believe rates will rise. Choose **variable rate** if you can absorb payment increases, want the lowest possible initial cost, or believe rates will stay stable or fall.

Consider your loan term carefully. Shorter terms mean higher monthly payments but dramatically lower total interest. Our [mortgage guide](/norway/mortgage-guide/) provides a detailed analysis of how term length affects total cost.

## Expert Tips for Norway

Get your finansieringsbevis before attending any viewings. Reduce existing debts (especially studielån) before applying — the 5x income debt cap is strictly enforced. Negotiate with your bank annually — Norwegian banks expect prutesamtaler (negotiation conversations) and often match competing offers. Choose floating rate unless you specifically want to lock in during a period of uncertainty
